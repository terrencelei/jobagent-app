Watch a company to receive new eligible full-time roles, with no minimum fit score. The first successful scan establishes a baseline; older listings are not emailed. JobAgent searches continuously: every active source is scanned again every 15 minutes (or as soon as a longer scan finishes) while this Mac and JobAgent are running. New eligible roles from watched companies arrive as one digest email per scan.

Just the company name is enough: the agent searches the web for its job board (about a minute), verifies it lists jobs, and saves it for future scans.

Email job alerts

Postings in LinkedIn, Handshake, ZipRecruiter and similar alert emails are added automatically. When a link needs a login or is a job-board copy, an agent finds the posting on the employer's own site and a browser reads it. Senders and limits are in Settings.
